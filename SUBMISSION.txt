THE PROBLEM

Everybody has opinions about stocks. Nobody has a record.

You tell your group chat you're long Nvidia. Six months later there is no proof you called it, no ranking, no rematch, and no way for anyone who believed you to follow you in. Tens of millions of people already know how to turn that argument into a game: draft a squad, face an opponent, watch the scoreboard. Nobody had built it for money you keep.

Outside the US it is worse: most retail investors cannot open a US brokerage account at all. They can hold tokenized US equities on Solana today, but there is nothing to do with them except stare at a number.

WHAT IT IS

A fantasy sports app where your roster is your real portfolio. Connect a Solana wallet, pick a sport — Football (11 slots), Basketball (5), American Football (9) — and fill each position with a tokenized US stock. The position decides the risk tier: the goalkeeper must be a blue chip, forwards must be momentum names. That constraint is the game: without it everyone buys the five hottest tickers and there is no contest. Don't own it yet? Buy it inline through Jupiter, signed in your own wallet.

Your wallet IS your squad — anything you hold that isn't in the starting lineup sits on the bench, ready to come on. There is no maximum squad size, because there is no maximum to what you're allowed to own.

HOW SCORING WORKS

Base points are ALPHA, not return. A pick scores on how far it beat SPYx, the S&P 500 tracker, over the same window — one point per 0.1% of outperformance. A stock that rises 2% while the market rises 2% scores nothing, so a rising market hands nobody points: the league measures stock picking, not timing.

Points bank on EVERY price tick — no weekly window, no "check back Sunday". The leaderboard moves while you watch it.

Substitutions and transfers are priced in opposite directions on purpose: subbing a bench stock in is free three times a day then costs points, while buying and selling is unlimited and free.

THE SOCIAL LAYER

Tap any name on the leaderboard and you see that manager: rank, points, league record, lineup, and their wallet. A Solana wallet is public, so none of this needs their permission.

FOLLOW them and you're told the moment they change their lineup — "Marcus subbed Tesla off, Palantir on."

ADOPT THEIR WALLET opens a sheet with every stock they hold, all ticked. Untick what you don't want, set one budget, buy the rest. Your money, your wallet, every swap signed by you — nothing moves between wallets and the manager isn't involved. It copies their PICKS, not their position sizes: the budget splits evenly, so you copy what someone thinks, not how rich they are.

BUSINESS MODEL

Basis points on every swap, taken through Jupiter's platform fee and collected inside the transaction the user already signs. No separate billing, no custody, no extra infrastructure. Drafting, transfers and adopt-wallet all create swaps, so revenue scales with a good manager's audience rather than our signup count.

WHY SOLANA, AND WHY THIS SURVIVES

Tokenized equities on Solana are real and growing — ~$5.8B in spot DEX volume in Q2 2026, up 114% quarter on quarter, and xStocks past $25B lifetime. More importantly they trade around the clock: a stock game on any other rail dies every night and weekend, so continuous scoring is only possible here. There is no custom smart contract — balances come from chain, prices and routing from Jupiter, and the user signs everything.

Fantasy stock trading has been killed twice; we designed around both. In 2015 the SEC shut down Stock Battle for unregistered security-based swaps; the fatal ingredient was a prize whose value depended on securities performance. Formation has no prize pool, entry fee or pot, and no money is ever pooled or moved between players — winning gets you standing. In June 2024 India's SEBI banned exchanges from sharing real-time price data with fantasy platforms; Formation has no exchange data dependency, so there is no feed to switch off.

WHAT IS BUILT

Working software, not slides. A Flutter Android app using Mobile Wallet Adapter, and a NestJS/Postgres backend running a continuous scoring engine, live at api.formation.titalabs.xyz. 100 tokenized stocks auto-refreshed from Jupiter, live prices, real on-chain balance reads, and real Jupiter swaps with the platform fee wired in, payable in USDC, SOL or SKR. Leaderboards with period filters, custom leagues, PvP, manager profiles, adopt-wallet, following and notifications. 74 unit, 15 integration and 6 end-to-end backend tests, plus 33 app tests.

Known gaps, plainly: Android-first, and event thresholds were calibrated for crypto volatility rather than equities, so they are still being tuned.

Our market is crypto-native retail investors outside the US. xStocks are Reg S and unavailable to US, Canadian, UK and Australian persons, so we serve the ~110 countries where they can be held — precisely where people cannot easily open a brokerage account.
