================================================================================
FORMATION
Fantasy football, but the players are real stocks and the squad is your wallet.
================================================================================


THE ONE LINE
------------

You draft a starting eleven of tokenized stocks, your points come from beating
the market, and anyone who likes your lineup can buy it for themselves in one
tap. You own every share the whole time.


THE PROBLEM
-----------

Everybody has opinions about stocks. Nobody has a record.

You tell your group chat you're long Nvidia. Six months later there is no proof
you called it, no ranking, no rematch, and no way for anyone who believed you to
follow you into it. Meanwhile tens of millions of people already know exactly
how to turn that argument into a game: draft a squad, face an opponent, watch
the scoreboard move, brag when you win.

Nobody has built that for real money you actually keep.

Outside the US the problem is worse. Most retail investors cannot open a US
brokerage account at all. They can hold tokenized US equities on Solana today,
but there is nothing to do with them except stare at a number.


WHAT IT IS
----------

A fantasy sports app where the roster is your real portfolio.

  1. Connect a Solana wallet.
  2. Pick a sport: Football (11 slots), Basketball (5), American Football.
  3. Fill each position with a tokenized US stock. Position decides risk tier:
     the goalkeeper must be a blue chip, forwards must be momentum names. That
     constraint is the game — it stops "just buy the five hottest tickers".
  4. Don't own it yet? Buy it inline through Jupiter, signed in your own wallet.
  5. Your points start moving on the next price tick.

Your wallet IS your squad. Anything you hold that isn't in the starting lineup
sits on the bench, ready to come on. There is no maximum squad size, because
there is no maximum to what you're allowed to own.

Nobody's money is ever pooled, escrowed, or moved between players. There is no
prize fund. The stake was always just what you already own, which is what makes
this a game rather than a gambling product wearing a costume.


HOW SCORING WORKS
-----------------

This is the part most people get wrong, so it's worth being precise.

Base points are ALPHA, not return. A pick scores on how far it beat SPYx, the
S&P 500 tracker, over the same window — one point per 0.1% of outperformance.
A stock that rises 2% while the market rises 2% scores nothing.

That single decision means a bull market doesn't hand everyone points. The
league measures stock picking, not luck with timing.

On top of that, each sport layers its own events, so the three modes actually
feel different rather than being reskins:

  Football           every +3% is a goal (a defender scoring is worth more than
                     a forward, exactly like the real game), clean sheets for
                     holding flat, conceded goals for dropping.
  Basketball         buckets for guards, blocks for the centre when it holds up
                     while the market falls, team double-doubles.
  American Football  touchdowns every +4%, receptions, turnovers, and a clean
                     pocket for a quarterback that never dipped 2%.

Football and basketball have a captain who scores a multiplier, with a
vice-captain as backup if the captain's pick doesn't count.

Points bank on EVERY price tick. There is no weekly window to wait out, no
"check back Sunday". The leaderboard moves while you're looking at it, and it
filters to all-time, this month, this week, or any custom range you pick.


SUBSTITUTIONS AND TRANSFERS
---------------------------

Two different things, priced differently, and the difference is deliberate.

  A SUBSTITUTION brings a bench stock into your starting eleven. Three free
  every day, then it costs you points. It's free for you and earns us nothing,
  so that's where the friction belongs.

  A TRANSFER is buying or selling. Unlimited and never charged in points.

If you sell a pick mid-window it is substituted off, not voided — it keeps every
point it earned up to the sale price, and a rally after you sold doesn't score
for you. Wanting out of a falling stock is a legitimate decision, and the game
shouldn't punish you for making it.


THE PART THAT GROWS
-------------------

Tap any name on the leaderboard and you see that manager: their rank, their
running points, their league record, their starting lineup, and their wallet.
A Solana wallet is public — none of this needs their permission.

Then two things you can do about it:

  FOLLOW them, and you're told the moment they change their lineup.
  "Marcus subbed Tesla off, Palantir on."

  ADOPT THEIR WALLET. A sheet opens with every stock they hold, all ticked.
  Untick what you don't want, set one budget, and buy the rest — your money,
  your wallet, each swap signed by you. Nothing transfers between wallets, and
  the manager isn't involved.

It buys their PICKS, not their position sizes. The budget splits evenly, so
you're copying what someone thinks, not how rich they are.

This is where the business is. A good manager with five hundred followers
generates five hundred purchases. Revenue scales with someone's audience rather
than with our signup count.


CUSTOM LEAGUES AND HEAD TO HEAD
-------------------------------

Create a league, public or private, and share the code. You choose when it
starts and how long it runs — an hour, a day, a week. Lineups lock when it
opens, so everyone is judged on the team they committed to, while the global
league keeps running underneath.

A head-to-head duel is the same object with two players in it. One code path,
one lifecycle, no special case.


HOW IT MAKES MONEY
------------------

Basis points on every swap, taken through Jupiter's platform fee and collected
inside the same transaction the user already signs. No separate billing, no
custody, no extra infrastructure — it's a parameter on a call we were making
anyway.

Every part of the product creates swaps:
  - drafting a team
  - transfers, which we deliberately never charge points for
  - adopting a manager's wallet, which is a basket of them at once

Secondary, later: subscription for multiple concurrent sport modes and deeper
analytics, and sponsored placement from tokenized-equity issuers who all want
distribution.


WHY SOLANA, AND WHY NOW
-----------------------

Tokenized equities on Solana are real and growing fast. Spot DEX volume for
tokenized stocks hit roughly $5.8B in Q2 2026, up 114% on the previous quarter,
and xStocks has passed $25B in total transaction volume while accounting for
about 86% of tokenized equity issuance on the network.

More importantly, they trade around the clock. A stock game on any other rail
goes dead every night and every weekend. This one never stops ticking, which is
the only reason continuous scoring is possible at all.

And there is no custom smart contract here. Balances are read from the chain,
prices come from Jupiter and Pyth, trades route through Jupiter, and the user
signs everything. That isn't laziness — it's why there's no custody risk to
defend.


WHY THIS SURVIVES WHERE OTHERS DIDN'T
-------------------------------------

Fantasy stock trading has been tried and killed twice. We designed around both.

  In 2015 the SEC shut down Stock Battle for offering unregistered
  security-based swaps. The fatal ingredient was a PRIZE whose value depended on
  how securities performed. Formation has no prize pool, no entry fee, and no
  pot. Winning gets you standing, not money.

  In June 2024 India's SEBI cut fantasy trading platforms off at the knees by
  banning exchanges from sharing real-time price data with them. Formation has
  no exchange data dependency — prices come from on-chain markets. There is no
  feed for anyone to switch off.

We also never take custody, never pool funds, and never trade on anyone's
behalf, which keeps us out of the adviser and broker-dealer perimeter that the
US copy-trading incumbents needed two years and two registrations to enter.


WHAT IS ACTUALLY BUILT
----------------------

All of the above is working software, not slides.

  - Flutter app on Android, wallet connect via Mobile Wallet Adapter
  - NestJS + Postgres backend, continuous scoring engine, price ticks
  - Live xStock prices and real on-chain balance reads
  - Real Jupiter swaps with the platform fee wired in
  - Leaderboard with period filters, custom leagues, PvP, manager profiles,
    adopt-wallet, following, and notifications
  - 54 unit, 15 integration and 5 end-to-end backend tests, 30 app tests

Known gaps, stated plainly: the app is Android-first, the sport modes beyond
football are lightly tuned, and the event thresholds were originally calibrated
for crypto volatility rather than equities and are still being adjusted.


WHO IT IS FOR
-------------

Crypto-native retail investors outside the United States. xStocks are issued
under Reg S and are unavailable to US, Canadian, UK and Australian persons — so
our market is the roughly 110 countries where they can be held, which is also
precisely where people cannot easily open a US brokerage account.

That is not a compromise. It is the only market where this product is both legal
and genuinely needed.


THE RISKS WE KNOW ABOUT
-----------------------

  Regulatory.  No prize means no security-based swap, and that line must hold.
               The moment a pot or a paid creator programme appears, the
               analysis changes completely.
  Market.      This is closer to a vitamin than a painkiller. Nobody is in pain
               from lacking a stock leaderboard. The social layer — having a
               public record and an audience — is what has to carry retention.
  Concentration. Solana's share of tokenized equity volume has fallen from ~97%
               to around 35% as competitors launched. We benefit from the
               category growing, not from one chain winning.
  Execution.   Making a leaderboard feel alive is a tuning problem, and we are
               still tuning it.
