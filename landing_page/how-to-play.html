The game

How to play

Football, basketball or American football. Same idea, three ways to play it.

In one line: your stocks are your players, and your team scores when its picks beat the market. Not when they simply go up, when they do better than the S&P 500.

01Your stocks are your players

Formation turns the tokenized stocks in your Solana wallet (xStocks, like AAPLx for Apple or NVDAx for NVIDIA) into a fantasy team. Already hold some? One tap lines them up for you. Don't hold any yet? Buy your first ones in the app, from a dollar. They go straight to your own wallet, never ours.

02Beat the market, don't just ride it

Every pick is measured against the market as a whole. When everything rises together, nobody scores; you score by picking the stocks that do better than the rest.

Your pick +2.0%, the market +1.0%+10 points

Your pick −1.0%, the market +0.5%−15 points

03Three kinds of player

Every stock has a tier, worked out from how it actually behaves, and every position on your team takes one tier. A good team mixes them, the same way a good side needs a defence as well as strikers.

Blue chip

Calm and steady

Barely follows the market's swings. Keepers and defenders, centres, tight ends and kickers.

Balanced

Moves with the market

The engine of the team. Midfielders, forwards in basketball, quarterbacks and running backs.

Momentum

Big swings

Where the goals come from, and the risk. Strikers, guards and wide receivers.

04How a week plays out

Points bank all through the week. Twelve match weeks make a season, and the season table is the one that counts.

Monday 9:30am ET

Kick-off. The match week begins.

During the week

Your picks score as prices move. Goals, buckets and touchdowns land as bonus events.

Friday 4pm ET

Full time. Results, head-to-head fixtures and the week's table settle.

The weekend

The transfer window: change your team as often as you like, for free.

Before a season starts it's pre-season: friendlies in football, preseason games in basketball and American football. Points still bank, so you can find your feet, but they don't count.

05You're the manager

Pick a captain

Your captain's points count extra in football and basketball, so choose who you back.

Use your bench

Every stock you hold but don't start waits as a substitute. A few free changes a day; more cost points.

Choose a shape

In football, pick your formation, from three at the back to five, to suit the stocks you hold.

06Leagues, rivals and bragging rights

Everyone plays in the global league. Start a private league with friends and you'll face one of them head to head every match week, with a proper league table. Or settle it in a duel. There's no entry fee and no prize pool: you play for the table.

07Kept fair

Every listed stock scores. Big jumps are checked before they count, and no single pick can run away with a day: each one's points are capped daily.

08An Assistant Manager in your dugout

Pick a character to be your Assistant Manager. Ask why you're losing points, get your squad reviewed, or let them draft your first team. They advise; you decide.

09Your wallet, your call

Your stocks never leave your wallet, and you approve every trade in it yourself. Buy and sell in the app whenever you like. Formation is a game of skill, not investment advice, and the value of any holding can fall as well as rise.
